Stencil is now available system-wide. Where to find it:

    Logic Pro       AU MIDI FX slot on a software-instrument track
    Bitwig Studio   Note FX slot, as VST3 or CLAP, before an instrument
    Reaper          VST3 or CLAP in any MIDI / FX chain
    Studio One      VST3 in the MIDI fx slot

Your DAW may need a plug-in rescan or restart before Stencil appears in the browser.

Stencil is a MIDI generator — chain it into a synth (or pair it with Pointsman for scale-locked output) to hear it.

Project: https://github.com/im9/stencil
Support: https://github.com/im9/stencil/issues

Thank you for installing Stencil.
