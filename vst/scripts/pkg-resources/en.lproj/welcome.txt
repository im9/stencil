Welcome to the Stencil __VERSION__ installer.

Stencil is a probabilistic MIDI sequence generator by im9 — a Music Thing-style Turing Machine shift register that emits notes from a controlled-randomness loop. This installer places the Stencil plug-in (VST3, AU, and CLAP formats) into the system-wide Audio Plug-Ins folders, so any DAW on this Mac can find it:

    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Stencil.vst3
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Stencil.component
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Stencil.clap

You will be asked for your administrator password before installation.

If you would rather place the bundles in a custom location, quit this installer and use the Stencil-__VERSION__.dmg distribution instead — it lets you drag the bundles wherever you want.

In the Customize step you can deselect any plug-in format you do not need (for example, skip CLAP on a Logic-only setup). All three are selected by default.

Stencil is a MIDI effect (note generator), not a synthesizer — it produces no audio on its own. Chain it into a downstream synth (or pair it with Pointsman for scale-locked output) to hear it.
